Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

SoundBase streams from your music server. The developer never receives your music, listening history or login. Optional features such as Google Cast and artwork search contact the services described below.

This policy explains how the SoundBase apps for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac (“SoundBase”, “the app”) handle information. SoundBase is free, open-source software. You can check everything described here in the source code.

1. Information the app handles

Your server address and login

To connect, you enter your server address, username and password. SoundBase does not store your password. It turns it into a salted token, the standard Subsonic authentication method, and stores only the server address, username and that token. They are kept on your device in the system Keychain on iPhone and Mac. They are shared with the SoundBase Apple Watch app over Apple's encrypted WatchConnectivity link so the Watch can sign in.

Your music library and listening activity

SoundBase sends requests directly from your device to your server to browse your library, stream and download music, fetch artwork and lyrics, save playlists and favourites, sync your play queue, and report plays (“scrobbles”). This information goes only to the server you chose. It is governed by that server's operator, who is usually you, and the developer never receives it.

Data stored on your device

Downloaded songs, cached artwork, your settings (for example theme and streaming quality) and a short list of plays waiting to be sent to your server while you're offline are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

2. Information the developer collects

None. The developer does not operate any servers for SoundBase and does not collect, store, sell or share personal information. SoundBase contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code written by the developer.

3. Third-party services

Google Cast (iPhone only)

The iPhone app includes Google's Cast SDK so you can play music on Chromecast, Google TV and Nest devices. To find and talk to Cast devices on your local network, the Cast SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • a device identifier
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information
  • product interaction data, such as how Cast features are used

Google says this data is used for app functionality and analytics. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. When you cast, the Cast device streams music directly from your server. Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The Apple Watch and Mac apps don't include the Cast SDK.

Library editing and artwork search

When you search for replacement album artwork, SoundBase sends the album and artist names to MusicBrainz. Cover previews and downloads contact the Cover Art Archive and its image hosts. Edits and imported images are sent to the helper on your own music server. The helper keeps recoverable originals and removed files for 30 days by default.

Your music server

Your server may use other services, such as Last.fm or similar sources for artist images, biographies and similar songs. Those are configured and controlled on your server, not by SoundBase.

Apple

If you download SoundBase from the App Store, Apple may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports, but only if you've chosen to share them with app developers. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

SoundBase doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live).

6. Your choices

  • Sign out in Settings to remove your server login from the device.
  • Delete downloads in Settings or Library › Downloaded.
  • Don't use casting if you'd rather the Cast SDK didn't run. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
  • Data held by your music server can be managed or deleted by that server's administrator.

7. Security

Use an https:// server address so traffic between the app and your server is encrypted. SoundBase can connect to plain http:// servers, for example on your home network, but that traffic isn't encrypted.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Its history is also visible in the project's public repository.
